# Sherah — AI Travel Fairness Companion

**Sherah** is an AI that sits on the traveler's side of every flight and hotel booking. Before you pay, it answers the four questions nobody else will:

1. **Is this price fair?** — a clean, anonymized re-check of your fare, plus a plain-language breakdown of hidden fees.
2. **When should I go?** — not just cheap dates, but the real reason behind them.
3. **Which company can I trust?** — a fairness score for any airline, hotel, or OTA.
4. **What if it's cancelled?** — your real rights under EU261, DOT, and the Montreal Convention, instantly.

## Live prototype

👉 **[Try it here](https://monarozzi.github.io/sherah/)**

This is an interactive prototype using simulated data — it demonstrates exactly how Sherah will reason once connected to live fare and rights data. Type in any route and any airline to see Sherah's four-pillar verdict. Available in English and Italian.
